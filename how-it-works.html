How it works

Find the money hiding in your hostel

Better Hosteling connects to the tools you already use, reads your numbers like a finance team would, and shows you exactly what to fix — and what it's worth.

1

Connect your tools

Link your booking system and bank or card in a couple of taps. Read-only — nothing changes without your approval.

2

AI finds the money

It spots cost leaks, benchmarks you against similar hostels, and surfaces upsells — each with a dollar value and a confidence level.

3

You approve, profit grows

Act on the ranked to-dos, track what you've recovered, and get a monthly profit report. The number only goes up.
